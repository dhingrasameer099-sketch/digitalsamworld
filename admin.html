CMS Role Permissions & Access Capabilities Matrix

Reference guide showing what each assigned user role can access across DigitalSamWorld CMS.

Administrator

Full unrestricted access to Core Settings, User Accounts & Roles, Permalinks, Pages, Blog Posts, Services, Homepage sections, and Leads Inbox.

Editor

Can create, edit, publish, and delete any Custom Pages, Blog Posts, Services, Case Studies, and moderate reader comments.

Author

Can write, edit, and publish their own Blog Posts & Playbooks on blog.html and manage their author profile.

Contributor

Can write and edit their own Blog Post drafts for editorial review, but cannot publish live or alter site settings.

Subscriber

Can log in, manage their personal user profile, and submit verified comments on blog posts without moderation delay.
